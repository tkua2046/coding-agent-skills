# Review the design

Read original requirements, confirmed clarifications, relevant code and the exact design version. Assess the shared [design contract](../assets/design.template.md), including a combined design/plan note when appropriate. Use the [review feedback contract and example](../assets/review.template.md) to report findings; review does not edit the design or product.

Judge the whole approach against the original goal, concrete behavior, consequences and usability at the selected depth. Unnecessary complexity can warrant changes when it creates material maintenance or review burden. Accept sufficient work without demanding separate documents, an alternatives catalog or stylistic changes. If independent review was requested, use a genuinely separate authorized context when available; otherwise disclose self-review.

On recheck, compare the relevant revision and evidence before revisiting findings. An unchanged verified resolution can be cited while it remains applicable. A changed contract, new evidence or incomplete prior verification requires checking the whole affected invariant; do not reopen unrelated decisions. Perform explicitly required rechecks even when content is unchanged.

After writing feedback, load the Review section of [artifact checks](../references/artifact-checks.md). Correct supported reporting defects without generating a self-review report or another round. Disclose unresolved issues.
