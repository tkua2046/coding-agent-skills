# Review or recheck a design

1. **Identify what is being reviewed.** Read the actual design revision, original requirements and relevant supplied code/evidence. On recheck, include the original finding and the affected revision; do not replay unrelated history. Review reports findings without editing the design or product.
2. **Judge the decision.** Assess whether the approach can satisfy the contract, explains consequential behavior/tradeoffs and has justified complexity. Examples should expose meaningful boundaries, including recovery where effects can persist. Accept an adequate local note; missing preferred headings or alternatives lists are not defects. A wrong supplied design can receive a correct, successful review when its material problem is identified.
3. **Resolve findings with evidence.** Give each material concern a location, trigger, consequence and sufficient correction/verification. Distinguish a blocker from optional polish. Preserve original findings and identify current dispositions: author-reported repair is not reviewer verification. Check the whole affected concern; partial fixes remain open and verified complete fixes close. Reuse applicable prior verification, performing explicitly required rechecks. State actual review mode and static/runtime limits; use an independent authorized context when independent review was requested, otherwise disclose the limitation.
4. **Finish for the reader.** Use the Review section of [final checks](../references/artifact-checks.md). Make the verdict and remaining material action easy to find, with supporting evidence beneath it. The [feedback example](../assets/review.template.md) is optional. Stop after a supported verdict; do not create another review to settle wording preferences.
