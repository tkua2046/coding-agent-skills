## Deferred rescoring boundary

Canonical rescoring is deferred following independent findings ECR-01 and the proposal review. This patch does not change release identity semantics. The following boundary is retained for any later proposal: the current `engine` identity covers the evaluator as well as execution. A new score may use a new evaluator on old observed execution, as ordinary offline evaluation does. Such a record must preserve the original source report and engine identity, reproduce the source's task/bundle/reader and complete phase checks, and state that execution was reused. It must not certify a changed worker runner. Any future release-gate treatment requires its own reviewed change.

A new scoring context receives the original task, generated artifacts and new rubric, without old grading replies, old verdicts or a preferred result. Rescoring must support both previous passes and failures, not select only favorable outputs. Changed worker-visible tasks, fixtures, overlays, skills or reader inputs need new execution. Historical reports are never edited or relabeled.

## Artifact responsibilities and fast behavioral feedback

The owner additionally requires general fixes without reward/rubric hacking, smaller LLM smoke tests, and explicit artifact responsibilities with flexible templates and post-generation checks. Keep the four skill entrypoints. Split responsibilities in the instructions and tests, not into more mandatory agents or workflow steps.

| Artifact | Owns | Does not duplicate | Post-generation question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Design | Intended behavior, boundaries, consequential choices and reasons, costs/failure consequences and an example | Execution progress, test inventories, line-by-line implementation | Can a reader understand the current decision and its consequence before historical context? Does detail resolve real uncertainty? |
| Implementation plan | Deliverable stage outcomes, dependencies, acceptance evidence and genuine gates | Design rationale, functions/tests enumerated in prose, copies of workflow policy | Can the next stage be executed and accepted? Would an internal refactor require rewriting this plan? |
| Review feedback | Reviewed revision/scope, actionable findings with consequence and verification, disposition of prior findings | A rewritten design/plan or unbounded preferences | Can the author act, and can a later reviewer tell what remains open and what was actually verified? |
| Delivery record | Current execution/review state and links to evidence | Live status copied into specs/design, repeated manifests, a committed record of its own commit hash | Can a fresh agent resume from one current state without rerunning valid work or claiming unperformed checks? |

Each operation loads only its relevant artifact contract and a short adaptable example. Author and reviewer use the same substantive contract; review-specific evidence/disposition rules stay in the review operation. Replace conflicting instructions rather than stacking new rules. Templates are aids, not required headings or extra files; a small change may combine design and plan while retaining these responsibilities. A self-check is silent unless it finds an issue, not another generated report.

Add a smoke tier to the existing evaluator: a small realistic input, one actual worker operation, one blind calibrated grader, saved original outputs and usage/time. Reuse the isolation/evidence machinery; do not create a second evaluation framework. Test meaningful paired behaviors (preserve a justified default vs expose a real ambiguity; accept sufficient work vs identify a consequential defect; close a verified fix vs retain a partial fix), plus design/plan ownership and truthful check reuse. Keep a transfer scenario out of the repair examples. Do not enforce wording, headings, document length or arbitrary counts as quality proxies.

Run the affected responsibility and adjacent responsibilities after a coherent prompt change, then affected complete canaries. Smoke results locate basic regressions; they cannot establish whole-workflow correctness or productivity. Keep historical failures and distinguish corrected measurement from improved skill behavior. Cost limits bound individual runs and trigger diagnosis; they do not replace completion criteria.
